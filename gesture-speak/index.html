Gesture to speech

Connect, and the page teaches itself: it shows one gesture at a time and you do it three times, with a short rest in between. No buttons. After the last one it keeps listening: every gesture you make is written down and spoken out loud, for as long as you stay connected.

Connect does nothing? Open this page through the web server: http://localhost:3000/gesture-speak/ in Chrome or Edge.

Double-tap your band and press Connect.
